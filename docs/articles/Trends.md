# Trends

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Trends — A Function Library**

- [`spline`](https://dd-harp.github.io/ramp.func/articles/Splines.html)
  — a generalized function family based on a trigonometric function.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Overall, a trend describes the general direction of change. In a
composed or decomposed time series, the trend is something like the
smoothed average across years. It is *not* the seasonal pattern, a
shock, or noise.
